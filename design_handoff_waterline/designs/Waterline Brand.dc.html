Waterline identity
Three refinements of the 1a mark, each shown at 64, 32 and 16px. The lockups, icons and splash below use 2c. The 16 and 32px favicons fall back to the solid 2a, because the gap closes up at small sizes.
2a
Solid
The original, with the water level lowered slightly so it reads as a gauge rather than a half-circle. Sturdiest at small sizes.
2b
Line through
The line runs past the glass, so the name is spelled out in the mark. Wider than square, which makes it harder to fit in icons.
2c · Recommended
Inset water
A gap between the glass and the water, like a tank seen from the side. It feels more precise and instrument-like. Below 24px it switches to 2a.

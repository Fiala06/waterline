## 1. Routine planted-tank cadences (water changes, filter, glass and substrate, trimming, CO2, root tabs, equipment)

### Takeaway
The routine is built around one weekly water change, usually 30–50% (high-tech or EI tanks lean toward 50%). Glass cleaning and trimming are usually done on the same weekly visit. Filter cleaning is less frequent (every 2–4 weeks up to monthly, or "when flow drops") and is deliberately kept off water-change day. Consumables and checks run on long cycles: root tabs every 2–3 months, CO2 cylinder refills every few months depending on size and bubble rate, and drop-checker fluid replacement every 2–3 weeks to every 2–3 months (sources disagree).

### Cited Findings
- Typical weekly tasks are water changes, glass cleaning and plant trimming, done together. — [planted-box.com, Aquarium maintenance per day, week and month](https://planted-box.com/en/blog/startup-maintenance/aquarium-maintenance/); [healthyaquariums.com, Maintenance schedule](https://healthyaquariums.com/guides/aquarium-maintenance-schedule/)
- A lightly stocked planted tank "may get by with 15 to 25 percent every one to two weeks". This is the low end, compared with the 50% weekly common in EI and PPS-Pro tanks. — [finsforgrins, How often should you do a water change](https://www.finsforgrins.com/post/how-often-to-do-water-changes-aquarium)
- EI and PPS-Pro both expect a 50% water change once a week (see section 2). — [PPS chemicals page](https://sites.google.com/site/aquaticplantfertilizer/home/chemicals); [Practical Fishkeeping, EI plant feeding](https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/features/what-you-need-to-know-about-ei-plant-feeding/)
- Filter: clean the mechanical media every 2–4 weeks, or only when flow drops ("typically every few water changes, not every one"). One canister keeper cleans monthly. Rinse media in tank water that was taken out, never in tap water (chlorine/chloramine kills nitrifiers). — [fishbazaarr, How often clean aquarium filter](https://fishbazaarr.in/blogs/aquarium-filters/how-often-clean-aquarium-filter); [plantedtank.net, When to clean a filter?](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/when-to-clean-a-filter.150509/); [fishlore, Water changes and filter maintenance on the same day?](https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/water-changes-and-filter-maintenance-on-the-same-day.235005/)
- Root tabs: "Refresh root tabs under heavy feeders every 2 to 3 months." — [forfishlovers, Planted tank guide](https://forfishlovers.com/planted-tank-guide/)
- CO2 cylinder: needs a refill "every few months". A 5 lb aluminium cylinder lasts about 2–4 months on a 40-gallon tank. The interval depends on tank size, bubble rate and light. — [2Hr Aquarist, CO2 system 101](https://www.2hraquarist.com/blogs/choosing-co2-why/co2-system-101); [Back Water Aquatics, CO2 install guide](https://backwateraquatics.com/how-to-install-a-co2-system-for-your-planted-aquarium-a-complete-guide/)
- Drop checker: the response lag is 1–2 hours by one source. Another says to let readings settle for 12 hours or more before changing anything. Indicator fluid replacement varies: every 2–3 months by one source, every 2–3 weeks by another (CO2Art), and "per product instructions" by a third. — [aquariumlesson, Drop checker guide](https://aquariumlesson.com/lessons/co2-drop-checker-guide/); [light.fish, CO2 drop checkers](https://light.fish/blog/co2-drop-checker); [CO2Art, Solution checker](https://www.co2art.us/blogs/blog/solution-checker-aquarium)
- CO2 should run on a timer or solenoid and be off at night. — [aquariumlesson, CO2 system guide](https://aquariumlesson.com/lessons/aquarium-co2-system-guide/)
- One guide warns against doing filter, substrate, decor and water work all on the same day: "change one major variable at a time". — [islandfishandreef, Why your maintenance schedule is wrong](https://www.islandfishandreef.com/blog/why-your-fish-tank-maintenance-schedule-is-wrong/); [tankzen, Maintenance schedule](https://tankzen.co/blog/aquarium-maintenance-schedule)

### Inferences
- The routine has three layers: (a) weekly, anchored on water-change day (WC, glass, trim, macro "reset"); (b) multi-week and set by condition (filter "when flow drops", usually every 2–4 weeks and kept off WC day); (c) long-cycle consumables (root tabs about 8–12 weeks, CO2 refill about 2–4 months, drop-checker fluid about 2 weeks to 3 months). Layer (c) is better shown as "due around…" or "when it runs out" than as a strict calendar.
- CO2 refill timing depends on use, so a log could estimate the next refill from the dates of past refills.

### Gaps
- No sources found on test-kit expiry cadence, light/fixture cleaning, impeller/O-ring service intervals, or substrate vacuuming cadence specifically in planted tanks (vacuuming is often skipped there because it disturbs aquasoil). Searches were not run on these for lack of budget.
- No ADA/Tropica/Dennerle primary maintenance schedules could be fetched (DNS failures).

## 2. Fertiliser dosing schedules and how they create weekday patterns tied to the water change

### Takeaway
EI produces a fixed 7-day rhythm: macros on 3 days (for example Mon/Wed/Fri), micros on the 3 alternate days (Tue/Thu/Sat), and one rest + 50% water-change day (Sun) that "resets" build-up. Dosing usually restarts right after the WC. PPS-Pro and all-in-one schemes are daily doses (often before lights-on) plus a weekly 50% WC. Lean dosing tends toward smaller doses and, in some retailer accounts, smaller or less frequent WCs. These conflict with the official PPS-Pro advice.

### Cited Findings
- EI: macros (KNO3, KH2PO4, K2SO4) Mon/Wed/Fri, micros (comprehensive trace including Fe) Tue/Thu/Sat, Sunday rest + 50% water change. Alternating is meant to avoid Fe–PO4 precipitation. Some hobbyists dose everything on the same days with no problem, but alternating "remains the standard recommendation". — [gensou.sg EI walkthrough](https://gensou.sg/?p=40746); [webmail.citizenactionwi.org mirror of EI guide](https://webmail.citizenactionwi.org/en/estimative-index-fertilization-method.html); [Practical Fishkeeping, EI](https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/features/what-you-need-to-know-about-ei-plant-feeding/)
- Sources differ on where the WC sits: either at the end of the cycle (Sunday) or at the start, with macros dosed straight after it. — [Barr Report thread, EI dosing amounts](https://mail.barrreport.com/threads/e-i-dosing-amounts-help.14737/); [UKAPS post](https://www.ukaps.org/forum/posts/201698/)
- EI targets per week: about 20–30 ppm NO3, 1–3 (or 3–6) ppm PO4, about 30 ppm K, about 0.5 ppm Fe. EI assumes CO2-injected, high-light tanks. Without CO2, excess nutrients "fuel algae". — [gensou.sg EI complete method](https://gensou.sg/?p=29451); [gensou.sg EI explained](https://gensou.sg/?p=20285)
- PPS-Pro: both solutions dosed daily, plus a 50% WC once a week, which holds about 28 ppm NO3 / 2.8 ppm PO4. Use half the dose for low light, the standard dose for medium, double for very high light. — [PPS chemicals & dosing](https://sites.google.com/site/aquaticplantfertilizer/home/chemicals); [plantedtank.net PPS-Pro thread](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/pps-pro-dosing-method.1285141/)
- Conflict: some third-party guides say PPS-Pro allows a water change only "once in a few weeks", or none unless TDS rises, or 20–30% weekly. — [aquarzon PPS Pro](https://www.aquarzon.com/supplies/315-pps-pro-fertilisers.html); [gensou.sg PPS-Pro guide](https://gensou.sg/pps-pro-dosing-method-guide/); [aquadolf EI vs PPS-Pro](https://aquadolf.com/estimated-index-ei-dosing-vs-pps-pro-fertilization-science/)
- Daily dosing is usually done before the lights come on. — [gensou.sg PPS-Pro guide](https://gensou.sg/pps-pro-dosing-method-guide/)
- Seachem Excel (liquid carbon) is dosed daily, with a larger dose after a water change. The search returned the product but not the label text (seachem.com unreachable). The general practice was confirmed only through retailer summaries: [smartaquariumguide, Flourish Excel dosing](https://smartaquariumguide.com/flourish-excel-dosing/)

### Inferences
- An app needs **weekday-patterned recurring tasks** (for example "Macros: Mon, Wed, Fri"), not only "every N days". It also needs a link to the WC day: if the keeper moves the WC from Sunday to Monday, the whole EI week moves with it (macros the day after the WC, micros alternating, rest day = WC day).
- PPS-Pro / all-in-one is "daily, morning" plus "weekly WC". That is two independent recurrences, but dose amounts may scale with a light-level setting.
- Dose times within a day matter (before lights-on), so a time-of-day hint helps.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch Tom Barr's original EI write-up, the 2Hr Aquarist "fertilizer 101" or the UKAPS EI explainer (DNS). A "lean dosing" schedule in the sense of Dennis Wong / 2Hr Aquarist was not found.
- Exact Seachem label wording (for example Excel "5 mL per 200 L after water change, then 5 mL per 200 L daily or every other day") was not checked in this session; the label needs checking.

## 3. Algae treatment regimens with concrete timings, by algae type

### Takeaway
Treatments fall into a few shapes. (a) A **fixed-length block**: blackout 3–5 days (max about 7), with a WC before and/or after and a short photoperiod (4–5 h) for 1–2 weeks after. (b) **Spot-treatment sessions**: syringe Excel or 3% H2O2 with flow off for about 5–30 min, repeated daily or every few days, then a WC. (c) **Elevated-frequency blocks**: daily double-dose Excel for 1–3 weeks, or WCs 2–3 times a week. (d) **Run until clear**: UV for green water, usually 3–5 days. Underneath each is a "fix the cause" change (CO2 stability, PO4 level, photoperiod) that is ongoing rather than time-boxed.

### Cited Findings
**Blackout**
- Usually 3–5 days, maximum about 7. Range given as 1–7 days depending on algae. Works on green water, cyanobacteria, most filamentous green algae and films; "almost totally inefficient against red algae" (BBA/staghorn). — [Aquasabi wiki, Black-out treatment](https://aquasabi.com/aquascaping-wiki/algae/black-out-treatment); [Green Aqua, Blackout](https://greenaqua.hu/en/blog/post/blackout-fight-against-algae-without-chemicals)
- During: block all light (including room light), keep aeration going, stop feeding, pause CO2 and liquid ferts. One protocol does a 50% WC at the start. After: photoperiod of 4–5 h for 1–2 weeks. — same sources as above (via search summary); [gensou.sg blackout](https://gensou.sg/?p=20567)
- Cyanobacteria blackout given as 3–4 days by one source and 3–5 days by another. A third warns that blackouts hurt plants as much as BGA, so they are a poor fit for planted tanks. — [Aquariadise BGA](https://www.aquariadise.com/blue-green-algae-aquarium/); [Buce Plant, Cyanobacteria](https://buceplant.com/blogs/aquascaping-guides-and-tips/how-to-get-rid-of-cyanobacteria-in-aquariums); [2Hr Aquarist, Cyanobacteria](https://www.2hraquarist.com/blogs/algae-control/how-to-control-cynobacteria)

**Liquid carbon (Excel/glutaraldehyde) spot and overdose**
- Spot: filter off, syringe Excel onto the algae, "let it bubble for a few minutes", then restart the filter. BBA turns pink/purple then white within 24–48 h. Repeat daily with water changes for stubborn growth. — [MonsterFishKeepers](https://www.monsterfishkeepers.com/forums/goto/post?id=6531954); [UKAPS post](https://www.ukaps.org/forum/goto/post?id=560398); [Green Aqua, Fighting algae with Excel](https://greenaqua.hu/en/blog/post/fighting-algae-seachem-flourish-excel)
- Local or "algae" dose cited as 1–2 mL / 40 L (normal or double). — [Green Aqua via search summary](https://greenaqua.hu/en/blog/post/fighting-algae-seachem-flourish-excel)
- Daily double dose: BBA gone in 2–3 weeks (anecdote). Double for one week also reported. Conflicts: the label warns against overdosing, and overdosing can harm plants (Vallisneria and others are commonly cited as sensitive), shrimp and filter bacteria (cloudiness). — [fishlore, BBA and Excel](https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/black-beard-algae-and-flourish-excel.64977/); [fishforums.net](https://www.fishforums.net/threads/blackb-beard-algae-and-overdosing-seachem-flourish-excel.352000/); [plantedtank.net, Excel as algaecide](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/flourish-excel-as-algacide-please-advise.1281189/)
- BBA returns unless CO2 stability is fixed. — [plantedtank.net, Excel, BBA and my plants](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/excel-black-beard-algae-and-my-plants.1301491/)

**Hydrogen peroxide (3%)**
- Spot rate commonly 1–2 mL per gallon applied by syringe. Whole-tank rate is about 1–2 mL per 10 gallons. Some go up to 3 mL/gal. For delicate livestock, no more than 1 mL/gal. — [Steenfott Aquatics](https://www.steenfottaquatics.com/blogs/aquarium-community-college/the-use-of-hydrogen-peroxide-in-controlling-algae-in-aquariums-mechanisms-dosage-and-effects); [Fin and Flux](https://finandflux.com/using-hydrogen-peroxide-kill-algae-dosage/); [plantedtank.net H2O2](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/hydrogen-peroxide.1016049/)
- Filter/flow off for about 5 min (one source) to 15–30 min (another) before restarting. Die-off within 24 h. Repeat until gone. Works on BGA, thread algae and soft films; little effect on GSA. — [sevenports](https://sevenports.com/2022/02/15/peroxide-treatment-for-algae-in-aquariums/); [aquascapeguide, H2O2 for hair algae](https://www.aquascapeguide.com/post/battling-the-green-menace-using-hydrogen-peroxide-for-hair-algae-control); [reef2reef, how often](https://www.reef2reef.com/threads/algae-spot-treating-w-hyd-peroxide-how-often-and-how-much.793615/)

**Cyanobacteria antibiotic / Chemiclean courses**
- Chemiclean (hobby guide): about 1 scoop per 10 gal, a 48-hour course, then a 25% WC. Not confirmed from the manufacturer label. — [gensou.sg Chemiclean](https://gensou.sg/cyanobacteria-treatment-chemiclean-guide/)
- Erythromycin (Maracyn) against BGA: whether it harms the biofilter is disputed. Test ammonia/nitrite during the course. It doesn't work on hair, staghorn or diatoms. — [aquariumsphere](https://aquariumsphere.com/get-rid-of-cyanobacteria-aquarium/); [schoolofscape](https://schoolofscape.com.au/blogs/blog/how-to-remove-cyanobacteria-in-aquariums)
- (Maracyn label course is in section 4.)

**Green water**
- UV sterilizer clears in "a matter of days". About 24–72 h for major clearing, full in 3–5 days. Forum reports of 3 days and of 4–5 days (water turns grey first). After clearing, one keeper cut UV run time to 4 h/day. — [Buce Plant, Green water](https://buceplant.com/blogs/aquascaping-guides-and-tips/how-to-get-rid-of-green-water-in-aquariums); [aquarium-guides UV guide](https://aquarium-guides.com/aquarium-uv-sterilizer-algae-guide/); [aquaticplantcentral, UV how long](https://www.aquaticplantcentral.com/threads/uv-sterilizer-and-green-water-how-long.11908/); [fishlore, UV first time](https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/using-uv-sterilizer-for-first-time-some-questions-about-it.528084/)
- UV must be paired with WCs, nutrient fixes and shorter light. — [Premium Aquatics, Green water](https://premiumaquatics.com/blogs/news/how-to-clear-green-water-in-aquarium)

**GSA, staghorn, hair algae**
- GSA is linked to low PO4 (<0.1 ppm) plus high light or low CO2. The fix is raising PO4 to about 0.5–1.0 ppm, plus a razor scraper on glass and nerite snails. — [aquariumbreeder, GSA](https://aquariumbreeder.com/how-to-remove-green-spot-algae-in-aquariums/); [Aquarium Co-Op, 6 common algae](https://www.aquariumcoop.com/blogs/aquarium/aquarium-algae)
- Staghorn: CO2 swings are the main cause of it coming back. The fix is spot-treating with liquid carbon plus CO2 stability. Grazers mostly ignore it. — [Back Water Aquatics, Staghorn](https://backwateraquatics.com/staghorn-algae-aquarium-causes-fix/); [fishlab, Staghorn](https://fishlab.com/staghorn-algae/)
- Hair/thread: manual removal (twist with a toothbrush), Amano shrimp at about 2–5 per 38 L, stable CO2, photoperiod about 6–8 h. One forum argues that high NO3/PO4 alone doesn't cause hair algae. — [2Hr Aquarist, Hair algae](https://www.2hraquarist.com/blogs/algae-control/hair-algae-planted-aquarium-causes-fixes); [CO2Art, Green hair algae](https://www.co2art.us/blogs/blog/how-to-prevent-the-growth-of-green-hair-algae); [fishlore PSA algae management](https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/psa-algae-management-in-the-planted-aquarium.405975/)

**Diatoms (new tanks)**
- Normal in the first weeks. Usually clear within 4–8 weeks as silicates run out and biofilm matures. — [2Hr Aquarist, Diatoms](https://www.2hraquarist.com/blogs/algae-control/how-to-remove-brown-algae-diatoms-planted-aquarium); [app-aquatic, Diatom timeline](https://app-aquatic.com/guide-diatoms-brown-algae-aquarium.html)

### Inferences
- Regimen shapes to model:
  1. **Block with fixed length plus before and after tasks**: blackout N=3–5 days, with WC at start (optional), lights off, CO2/ferts/feeding paused, then WC on the day lights return, then reduced photoperiod for 7–14 days.
  2. **Repeated session with a short in-session timer**: spot dose, then flow off for X min (5–30), then flow back on. Repeat daily or every other day until resolved, with a WC the next day or at the next scheduled WC.
  3. **Dosing escalation for N weeks**: daily Excel ×2 for 1–3 weeks, then back to normal dose.
  4. **"Until clear" with a soft cap**: UV on until water clears (typically 3–5 days), then reduced or off. Blackout capped at 7 days.
- A regimen often **pauses or changes routine tasks** (CO2, ferts and feeding paused during blackout; photoperiod reduced afterwards). A model needs "suspend recurring task X during regimen" as well as adding new tasks.
- Ends are **condition-based** ("algae turns white", "water clear", "nitrate back in range") more often than date-based, so a regimen probably needs an "end early / extend" action.

### Gaps
- No manufacturer label text for Chemiclean, Seachem Excel or API Algaefix could be fetched, so official durations and repeat intervals are unconfirmed.
- No reliable timings found for "2–3 WCs per week for N weeks" as an algae-specific regimen beyond new-tank startup (section 4). Widely repeated in the hobby but not sourced here.
- Photoperiod "ramp back up" increments (for example +30 min per week) were not found in sources.

## 4. Other temporary regimens: medication, quarantine, fishless cycling, new-tank startup, vacation

### Takeaway
Medication labels use fixed day-indexed courses (Maracyn: dose daily on days 1–5, 25% WC on day 6, no carbon, optional repeat once). Other brands differ (API E.M.: 4 daily doses with a 25% WC before each dose). Fishless cycling is daily or periodic dosing plus testing until a condition is met (2 ppm ammonia gone to 0 within 24 h, nitrite 0), then a large WC. New planted tanks get 50% WCs 2–4 times a week for the first weeks, tapering by about 2 months. Vacation prep is a sequence counted back from the departure date, and catch-up is a sequence counted forward from the return date.

### Cited Findings
- Maracyn (Fritz, label): days 1–5 one packet per 10 gal every 24 h. Day 6 a 25% WC. Repeat the 5-day course once if improved but not cleared. Avoid WCs during treatment. Remove activated carbon. — [Fritz Maracyn product page](https://fritzaquatics.com/products/mardel-maracyn); [DailyMed label](https://fda.report/DailyMed/e05fc13b-1317-48f7-82c6-c318b660cf52)
- API E.M. Erythromycin: dose every 24 h for 4 consecutive days, with a 25% WC before each daily dose. Different from Maracyn, so "don't mix the two schedules". — [furrycritter, API E.M.](https://www.furrycritter.com/pages/pharma/fish/api_em_erythromycin.htm); [Aquarium Co-Op forum, API vs Maracyn directions](https://forum.aquariumcoop.com/topic/26076-api-erythyromycin-vs-fritz-maracyn-directions-discrepancy/)
- Forum conflict: some hobbyists run erythromycin for 10–14 days with a daily 50% WC before redosing, which contradicts the label. — [plantedtank.net, Water changes during Maracyn](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/water-changes-during-maracyn-dosing.497169/)
- Fishless cycling: dose about 2 ppm ammonia (some use 5 ppm, and too much can stall the cycle). The cycle is done when 2 ppm clears to 0 ammonia/0 nitrite within 24 h. Then do a large WC to bring nitrate below about 10 ppm. A minority warns that an extreme WC can cause a mini-cycle. — [Aquarium Co-Op forum, how to know cycle is done](https://forum.aquariumcoop.com/topic/36491-how-to-know-when-cycle-is-done-completely); [thinkfish, Started fishless cycle](https://forums.thinkfish.co.uk/fishtank-filtration-and-cycling/started-fishless-cycle); [capitalcichlids](https://www.capitalcichlids.org/forums/threads/question-about-fishless-cycle.9065)
- New planted tank: 3–4 WCs in the first week, then weekly 50%, tapering after about 2 months (Aquarium Gardens). Another guide says 50% 2–3 times a week for the first weeks. Aquasoil leaches nutrients and silicates for several weeks. — [Aquarium Gardens, Fight against algae](https://www.aquariumgardens.co.uk/the-fight-against-algae-38-w.asp); [Back Water Aquatics, Algae in new aquarium week by week](https://backwateraquatics.com/algae-in-new-aquarium-first-8-weeks/); [2Hr Aquarist, Algae and tank maturity](https://www.2hraquarist.com/blogs/algae-control/algae-and-tank-maturity)
- Vacation prep: a 20–25% WC about 3 days before leaving, then a larger WC and final adjustments the day before. One last fertiliser dose the day before (plants are fine for under 2 weeks without dosing). Shorten the photoperiod (6–7 h, or as little as 4 h for a fully planted tank). — [theaquariumhome, Prep for vacation](https://theaquariumhome.com/2024/11/26/how-to-prep-your-aquarium-for-vacation/); [aquaticplantcentral, Vacation question](https://www.aquaticplantcentral.com/threads/vacation-question.71504/); [2Hr Aquarist, Leaving for a holiday](https://www.2hraquarist.com/blogs/hot-topics/preparing-a-planted-tank-before-leaving-for-a-holiday)
- CO2 during vacation is disputed. Turn it off for trips over a week (pH-crash risk), or keep it running (one forum says pH rise breaks EDTA chelation; another keeper gets algae whenever CO2 is off). — [aquaticplantcentral, High-tech on vacation](https://www.aquaticplantcentral.com/threads/best-practice-for-high-tech-planted-tank-when-going-on-vacation.90048/); [UKAPS, Long vacation](https://www.ukaps.org/forum/threads/planted-tank-going-on-a-long-vacation.67135/)
- After returning: a 25–30% WC, trim melted or dead leaves, resume feeding gradually, and bring light and ferts back over several days. — [theaquariumhome](https://theaquariumhome.com/2024/11/26/how-to-prep-your-aquarium-for-vacation/); [Canton Aquatics, Going on vacation](https://www.cantonaquatics.com/blogs/guide-to-aquascaping/going-on-vacation-how-to-keep-your-planted-tank-alive-while-youre-away-1)

### Inferences
- Medication courses map cleanly to **day-indexed templates** ("Day 1–5: dose; Day 6: WC 25%; optional repeat"), with a **pre-condition task** (remove carbon) and a **post task** (put carbon back). The ordering within a day matters (API E.M.: WC *before* the dose).
- Fishless cycling is a **daily dose + test loop with an exit condition** tied to test readings: 0 NH3 / 0 NO2 within 24 h, then a large WC. Test readings that the app already stores could close the regimen.
- New-tank startup is a **tapering frequency** (several times a week, then weekly), and diatoms are expected and self-limiting (4–8 weeks).
- Vacation is a pair of **date-anchored sequences**: tasks at departure −3 d and −1 d, and tasks at return +0 d over a few days. Routine tasks are suspended in between.

### Gaps
- No quarantine-tank schedules were searched (budget). Typical 2–4 week QT durations and prophylactic course sequences are unsourced here.
- No manufacturer schedules for other common planted-tank-safe meds (for example Seachem Paraguard, API General Cure) were collected.

## 5. Why keepers shift maintenance days, and which tasks must or must not coincide

### Takeaway
Most-cited rules: **don't clean the filter on the same day as a large water change** (space them about 1–2 weeks apart, or at least on different days) and don't deep-clean several systems at once. **Dose macros right after the WC** (EI reset). For spot treatments, **apply with flow off, restore flow, then WC** at the next scheduled change or the next day. With medication, **WC before redosing** (API) or **no WC until the course ends** (Maracyn). No sourced material was found on scheduling around busy weeks or weekends specifically.

### Cited Findings
- Filter cleaning and WC on different days. Several guides say to space them by at least 1–2 weeks so bacteria aren't disturbed at the same time as a big water change. If both must happen together, only rinse the mechanical media. — [fishlore thread](https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/water-changes-and-filter-maintenance-on-the-same-day.235005/); [healthyaquariums](https://healthyaquariums.com/guides/aquarium-maintenance-schedule/); [fishtankworld](https://www.fishtankworld.com/clean-fish-tank/)
- Don't do filter, substrate, decor and water all on one day. Change one variable at a time. — [islandfishandreef](https://www.islandfishandreef.com/blog/why-your-fish-tank-maintenance-schedule-is-wrong/)
- EI: macros dosed straight after the WC by some, and the weekly WC is the reset. — [Barr Report thread](https://mail.barrreport.com/threads/e-i-dosing-amounts-help.14737/)
- Excel spot dosing with "repeated daily treatments with water changes". H2O2 followed by a normal Excel dose was reported to work. — [Green Aqua](https://greenaqua.hu/en/blog/post/fighting-algae-seachem-flourish-excel); [plantedtank.net, Excel as algaecide](https://www.plantedtank.net/threads/flourish-excel-as-algacide-please-advise.1281189/)
- Medications: Maracyn says no WC during the course; API E.M. says WC before each dose. — [Fritz Maracyn](https://fritzaquatics.com/products/mardel-maracyn); [furrycritter API E.M.](https://www.furrycritter.com/pages/pharma/fish/api_em_erythromycin.htm)
- Filter cleaning is often set by flow rather than the calendar ("only when flow drops"). — [fishbazaarr](https://fishbazaarr.in/blogs/aquarium-filters/how-often-clean-aquarium-filter)

### Inferences
- "Staggering" can be modelled as **two recurrences with an offset** (WC weekly on Sunday; filter every 4 weeks on a mid-week day) or as a **conflict rule** ("not on the same day as WC; if they coincide, push the filter to the next day").
- "Dose macros after WC" and "WC before medication dose" are **same-day ordering** dependencies. "WC the day after spot treatment" and "WC on day 6 after course" are **next-step** dependencies.
- When a keeper moves the WC (busy day), EI-patterned dosing should move relative to it, and a staggered filter task should keep its offset from the WC rather than land on it.

### Gaps
- No sources quantify how often keepers actually shift days, or why (busy schedules, weekends, waiting on test results). This is folk practice that I couldn't find written down. Forum-thread mining or user research would be needed.
- Big-trim timing relative to the WC (many trim *before* the WC so debris is siphoned out) is common hobby practice but was not sourced in this session.

## 6. Condition-triggered and seasonal tasks

### Takeaway
Condition triggers seen in sources: **filter clean when flow drops**; **WC when nitrate is high** (bring NO3 below about 10 ppm after cycling); **water change when TDS rises** (PPS-Pro variants); **end fishless cycle when 2 ppm NH3 clears to 0 in 24 h**; **raise PO4 when GSA appears and PO4 < 0.1 ppm**; **refill CO2 when the cylinder runs out**. No sourced seasonal-maintenance guidance was found.

### Cited Findings
- Filter cleaning by flow rather than calendar. — [fishbazaarr](https://fishbazaarr.in/blogs/aquarium-filters/how-often-clean-aquarium-filter)
- WC triggered by nitrate after fishless cycling (target below 10 ppm). — [thinkfish](https://forums.thinkfish.co.uk/fishtank-filtration-and-cycling/started-fishless-cycle); [Aquarium Co-Op forum](https://forum.aquariumcoop.com/topic/36491-how-to-know-when-cycle-is-done-completely)
- PPS-Pro variant: no WC "unless TDS rises significantly". — [gensou.sg PPS-Pro](https://gensou.sg/pps-pro-dosing-method-guide/)
- Cycle completion criterion: 2 ppm ammonia reaches 0 within 24 h. — [Aquarium Co-Op forum](https://forum.aquariumcoop.com/topic/36491-how-to-know-when-cycle-is-done-completely)
- GSA trigger: PO4 < 0.1 ppm, so raise to 0.5–1.0 ppm. — [aquariumbreeder GSA](https://aquariumbreeder.com/how-to-remove-green-spot-algae-in-aquariums/)
- Drop-checker colour (CO2) readings should settle 12 h or more before you act on them. — [light.fish](https://light.fish/blog/co2-drop-checker)
- In the Wikipedia overview, algae type is linked to cause (useful for mapping "observed algae → regimen"). — [Wikipedia, Freshwater aquarium algae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freshwater_aquarium_algae)

### Inferences
- An app that already stores test readings and targets (Waterline's `status.ts`) could suggest regimen tasks when a reading crosses a threshold (NO3 high → WC; PO4 very low → raise PO4 dose; NH3/NO2 > 0 in a cycled tank → WC + test daily). Most keepers act on such triggers by hand today.
- "Observed X" (an algae type logged as a note or tag) could map to a suggested regimen template, but the sources above conflict often enough that templates should be editable defaults, not prescriptions.

### Gaps
- No sources found on seasonal changes (for example heater checks, temperature-driven CO2 or photoperiod adjustments, summer evaporation top-off cadence). Not searched for lack of budget.
- No evidence collected on how often keepers really test (weekly vs before WC vs only when there's a problem).
